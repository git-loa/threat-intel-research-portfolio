# What is Phishing 
- Phishing is a type of cyberattack where threat actors masquerade as legitimate companies or individuals to trick people into giving away sensitive information like password, banking details, or personal data.

- The attackers send messages that look real -emails, texts, calls, or even social media DMs.

- The goal is to make people click a malicious  link, download a harmful file, or hand over private information.

- It is a form of social engineering, exploiting emotions like urgency, fear, curiosity, ot trust.

- Phishing works because it targets **human psychology**, not just the technology.
    - We trust familiar brands.
    - We react quickly to urgency.
    - We want to fix problems immediately.
    - We don't inspect links or senders. datails.

## Types of Phishing
Major variants to encounter in CTI work:

| Type | Description |
|----|------------|
| Email phishing | Fake emails pretending to be from trusted organizations. |
| Smishing | Phishing via SMS/text messages. |
| Vishing	| Voice phishing—phone calls pretending to be banks, government, etc. | 
| Spear phishing |	Highly targeted messages aimed at specific individuals. |
| Whaling	| Targeting executives or high-value individuals. |
| Clone phishing	| Attackers copy a legitimate email and replace links/attachments. |
